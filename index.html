2. Rules and regulations

Read these requirements carefully before checking in.

  1. Ensure all required documents, including the PCF and applicable parent/guardian consent forms, have been submitted.
  2. Obtain the required medical clearance issued by the school clinic before participating.
  3. Arrive at the designated boarding or assembly area on time and follow the event schedule.
  4. Check that you have all personal belongings and required items before leaving the area.
  5. Follow instructions from authorized AKPC officers, event staff, and school personnel.
  6. Keep your boarding pass accessible and do not share it with another person.
  7. Maintain respectful conduct, cleanliness, and care for school facilities and equipment.
  8. Promptly inform an authorized officer about medical concerns, missing documents, or any issue affecting your participation.
